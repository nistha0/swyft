SWYFT - How to Add Custom Music
==================================================

To add your own music to the game:

1. Place your music files in this 'music' folder
2. Supported formats: .mp3, .ogg, .wav
3. Name your files:
   - For MENU music: Include 'menu' in the filename
     Example: menu_theme.mp3, background_menu.ogg
   - For GAMEPLAY music: Include 'game' or 'play' in the filename
     Example: gameplay1.mp3, game_music.ogg, play_theme.wav

4. Restart the game to load your music!

Notes:
- The game will randomly select from available tracks
- Music loops automatically
- You can have multiple tracks for variety
- If no music files are found, the game runs silently

Recommended free music sources:
- incompetech.com (Kevin MacLeod)
- freemusicarchive.org
- bensound.com
- youtube.com/audiolibrary

Enjoy your game with music!
